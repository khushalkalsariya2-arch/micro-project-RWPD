Our story

A family guest house that grew up

Bluetide began in 2012 as a nine-room guest house run by the Desai family. Today it has 42 rooms, but the same family still eats lunch in the staff canteen.

We built the hotel for people driving down from Surat, Vapi and Mumbai for a quiet weekend. That is why the rooms are simple, the pool faces the sunset and the price you see is the price you pay. The GST is shown before you confirm, never after.

The place

Between the garden and the sea

The hotel sits on Devka Beach Road in Nani Daman. Garden rooms open onto the lawn; the upper floors look over the trees to the water.

Hotel policies

The rules of the house

  • Check-in is from 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM.
  • Every adult shows one original at the desk.
  • Early check-in is free when a room is ready. Late check-out until 4 PM costs ₹800.
  • Cancel free up to 24 hours before check-in, from the My bookings page.
  • After that, one night's room rate is charged.
  • If you do not arrive, the booking is released at 11 AM the next day.
  • Pay at check-in by cash, card or UPI. No advance is taken online.
  • is added to the room rate and shown before you confirm.
  • The offer code TIDE15 takes 15% off the room rate, not off extras.
  • Children under 12 stay free in their parents' room, using the existing beds.
  • An extra bed or a baby cot is free on request, if the room has space.
  • Pets are welcome in Garden Cottages only. Please tell us in the special requests box.
