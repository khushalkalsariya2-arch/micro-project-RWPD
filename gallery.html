Things to do

Six ways to spend a day here

Activities run by the hotel team. Book any of them at the front desk or add a note to your reservation.

Sunrise swim

The pool opens at 6 AM. Tea and biscuits are set out on the deck until 8.

Garden and beach walk

A 40-minute guided walk through the garden and down to Devka Beach.

Evening wind-down

Housekeeping draws a warm bath with sea salt while you are at dinner.

Mocktail hour

Learn three drinks from our bartender, then take the recipes home.

Coastal cooking class

Cook a Daman-style fish curry and a vegetarian undhiyu with the chef.

Saturday live music

A local band plays in the banquet hall on Saturday nights, with a buffet dinner.
